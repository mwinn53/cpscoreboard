Posting to X

Posts about the watched teams during a round, like the legacy bot: when a team appears on the board, live score and place changes while it's actively playing, an hour left and time up, then periodic place changes once it's done (time up, or no play for 20 minutes) as other teams finish. Dry run writes the posts to the log below without sending them, so you can see what would go out. Limits apply to real posts only; anything over a limit is skipped and logged.
