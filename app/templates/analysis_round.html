Scores {% call tip("tests-help", "How the tests work") %} Scores are lopsided, with ties and outliers, so the comparisons use ranks rather than averages. "A random GA team outscored a random team from elsewhere 58% of the time" is the effect size: 50% would mean no difference. The 95% CI (confidence interval) is the range the true value plausibly lies in, from resampling the teams (bootstrap). The p-value (Mann–Whitney test) is how often chance alone would produce a difference this large; below 0.05 counts as statistically significant. Our own 3–4 teams are too few to test within one round; the season pages test them across rounds (each team-round counted once, so treat those results as a guide). Correlations are rank correlations (ρ) with their p-values. {% endcall %}
