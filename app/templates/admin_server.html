Shut down

Once the season is over and the public scoreboard is gone, there's nothing left to collect. Shutting down:

  1. takes a final snapshot of the data volume (database and season archive), then
  2. stops the server. The site goes offline, and you stop paying for the server itself; the disk, its snapshots and the IP address cost a few dollars a month. It can be started again later.

Download the archive first if you want a copy of your own.
